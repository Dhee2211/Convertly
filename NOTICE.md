# Third-party notices

Convertly runs entirely in your browser. The only external requests are for live exchange rates (currency converter only).

| Service | Use | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Exchange Rate API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com) (open.er-api.com) | Primary source of exchange rates. Attribution is shown on the currency page. | https://www.exchangerate-api.com/terms |
| [Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev) | Fallback source (European Central Bank rates) | https://frankfurter.dev |

City and time zone data come from the browser's built-in `Intl` API (IANA time zone database).
